About Me

I am a Ph.D. student in Economics at HSBC Business School, Peking University. My research focuses on the role of multinational firms, structural transformation, and digitalization in economic development. I employ both structural modeling and reduced-form empirical strategies to investigate the social and economic impacts of technology in emerging markets.
