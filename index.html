About Me

I am a Ph.D. student in Economics at HSBC Business School, Peking University. My research lies at the intersection of development economics and international economics, with a particular focus on the role of multinational firms, structural transformation, and digitalization in economic development. I study how the expansion of multinational enterprises and the diffusion of digital technologies reshape production structures, labor markets, and productivity dynamics in developing economies. My work combines empirical methods with structural approaches to quantify these effects and to better understand the mechanisms through which globalization and digital transformation contribute to economic growth and welfare.
